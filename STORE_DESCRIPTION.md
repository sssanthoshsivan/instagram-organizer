## Full description (store listing, ~1,000 chars)

### InstaBulk — Bulk Manage Your Instagram Saved Posts

**InstaBulk** is a Chrome extension that helps you manage your Instagram saved posts in bulk.

Once you're on your Saved page, InstaBulk adds a floating toolbar that lists all of your selected saved posts. You can:

- **Select multiple posts** with one-click checkboxes.
- **Unsave selected posts** in bulk with a single click.
- **Move posts to collections** (stub — planned for a future release).
- **Track progress** with a real-time counter of completed, failed, and remaining posts.
- **Keep your selection** after navigating between pages (selection persistence).

### Why InstaBulk?

Managing dozens of saved posts one-by-one on Instagram is tedious. InstaBulk lets you:

- Clean up your saved library quickly.
- Batch-remove posts you no longer need.
- Reorganise your saved content without leaving the page.

### Features

- ✅ Bulk unsave any number of saved posts at once
- ✅ Real-time progress counter (completed / failed / remaining)
- ✅ Checkbox selection on saved post tiles
- ✅ Selection persistence across page navigation
- ✅ Clean, minimal toolbar that stays out of the way

### Privacy

InstaBulk is a **local-only** extension. It does not:

- Send your data to any remote server.
- Track you or collect personal information.
- Access anything outside Instagram's `saved` page.

All extension state (bulk mode, selected posts) lives in Chrome's `chrome.storage` and never leaves your browser.

### Requirements

- Google Chrome (Manifest V3)
- An Instagram account with saved posts
